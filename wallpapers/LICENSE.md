# RepoVibe wallpapers — origin and license

All 20 wallpapers in this folder (and their thumbnails in `thumbs/`) are **original artwork created for RepoVibe**.
They are procedurally painted illustrations rendered by `scripts/wallpapers/scenes.js` (scene code) and
`scripts/wallpapers/generate.js` (deterministic renderer: fixed seeds, 1920×1080 WebP, 480×270 thumbnails).

- No third-party images, photographs, fonts-as-art, characters or copyrighted artwork were used or traced.
- "Anime-inspired" scenes are generic settings (cherry-blossom street, hillside village, cozy room); they contain
  no characters and do not reproduce any existing work.
- The Digital rain scene draws generic numerals, Latin letters and half-width katakana glyphs from the system
  monospace font at render time; it is not derived from any film's assets.
- License: distributed under the same license as RepoVibe. Attribution: "RepoVibe contributors".

Regenerate (byte-identical given the same Chromium version): `npm run wallpapers`.
