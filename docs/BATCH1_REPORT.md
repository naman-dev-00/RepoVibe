## Messy Thoughts
* **What it is**: a still figure in clean white outline on black; the head is a tangle of scribbled lines with a few wisps escaping.
* **How it is made**: vector paths generated by `scripts/wallpapers/messy-thoughts.js` (seeded maths, original). The page draws them as
  inline SVG, so the lines are resolution-independent: at 3840 × 2160 they are rendered at that size, not enlarged.
  17 resting strokes (figure + tangle) and 17 live strokes.
* **Motion**: each live stroke draws itself, rests and fades (or runs on through and off its end), on its own duration (11–27 s) and
  phase; the tangle as a whole breathes by under 2° and 2 %. The figure has no animation at all. Static and reduced motion show the
  complete drawing with every stroke finished.
* **Measured** (artwork only, 2560 × 1440, Ambient): about 6 % of pixels change between frames 2.5 s apart.

## Eternal Moon
* **What it is**: an enormous full moon over a spruce forest and a mirror lake, in greys, silver and black.
* **How it is made**: painted by `scripts/wallpapers/eternal-moon.py` at **3840 × 2160** (original): moon with albedo mottling, maria,
  about 1,500 craters and limb darkening; about 290 spruces whose boughs are solid shapes with ragged needle hems, plus bare trees;
  the lake mirrors the forest and moon with ripple displacement and a glitter path; about 4,000 stars.
* **Motion**: the moon, trees and lake never move. Thin cloud drifts across the sky; three fog planes slide in opposite directions,
  each confined to its own band (behind the far trees, between the tree ranks, over the lake); nine stars twinkle; the halo and the
  light in the fog breathe slowly.
* **Measured** (artwork only, 2560 × 1440, Ambient): 10–24 % of pixels change between frames 3 s apart.
* **Honest limits**: this is stylised silhouette art, not a photograph. At 100 % on a 4K screen the moon's surface is plausible but
  generic, and the needle hems are visibly drawn shapes. If the reference is photographic or painterly in a way this is not, say so.

## Aurora (redesigned from the supplied reference)
* Frozen fjord valley: snow-streaked crags lit cyan from the left and pink from the right, a teal → blue → violet sky with about 2,600
  stars, a glowing cracked ice lake, foreground rocks. Live: three flowing ribbons recoloured to teal / cyan / violet, three breathing
  veils behind them, ground mist, ice glow and glints, twinkling stars. Cabin and pines removed.
* **Honest assessment against the reference**: palette, composition and mood follow it; **detail and rendering do not reach it**. The
  reference is a painterly illustration with soft gradients and fine rock texture; this is flat cel shading drawn by code, the
  foreground rocks are lighter and simpler than the reference's, and the snow bank is a plain shape. Its layers are 2560 × 1440, so a
  4K screen enlarges them 1.5×. If that gap matters, Aurora belongs on the master-art route too.

## Why reference-based scenes lost quality (findings)
1. **The references are small**: 768 × 512, 768 × 512, 736 × 1476 and 740 × 416 px. A 2560-px screen needs them enlarged 3.3–3.5×,
   a 4K screen about 5×; that cannot create detail.
2. **So every reference-based scene was redrawn by code** (shapes, gradients, noise). That substitution is the main cause of the
   artificial look.
3. **Not the cause**: blur. Scene layers carry no blur filter; panel blur exists only in the opt-in Frosted Glass appearance.
4. **Secondary**: all earlier layers are 2560 × 1440 and are enlarged 1.5× on 4K screens.
5. **Correction**: an earlier note said pointer parallax still existed. It does not. There is no pointer listener in the content
   script, an existing browser test sweeps the pointer and asserts nothing reacts, and the new worlds are covered by the same check.

## What changed in the pipeline
* **Master-art intake** (`scripts/wallpapers/import-master.py`, spec in `docs/MASTER_ART.md`): checks rights, size, layer registration
  and whether the image was enlarged from a smaller one; crops to 16:9 without stretching; encodes without repainting, filtering or
  upscaling. Verified on real data in check mode: a native 4K image passes (detail score 0.43), the 740-px Aurora reference enlarged
  to 3840 px is refused (0.99), missing rights are refused. **Its write path has not been run on real master art yet.**
* **Asset tiers**: worlds with 4K layers also ship 2560-wide variants. The page uses 4K only when the screen can show more than 2560
  device pixels, and swaps up if the window grows.
* **Vector sprites**: strokes rendered as inline SVG.
* **Existing users**: adding presets does not change what any repository resolves to. The automatic pool is fixed at the original
  twenty presets (unit-tested against the old rule for 400 repositories); the new worlds are never picked automatically.
